About Arcus Design Build

What makes us different?

Most projects run through four or five separate consultants. At ADB the architect, the interior designer, the MEP engineers and the project manager sit in one studio, so one brief becomes one set of coordinated drawings and one accountable partner on site.

Arcus Design Build is based in Noida and works across India. We integrate sophisticated architectural vision with robust engineering to create high-performing commercial and residential environments: food courts, restaurants and cafés, retail, offices, homes and highway destinations.

The studio is led by Principal Architect Ankit Pandey, who brings more than fifteen years of experience across corporate, residential, retail and hospitality projects, along with international compliance auditing and consultancy across Japan and South Korea. Our approach is data-driven and aesthetic-led: the latest design technologies and sustainable materials, delivered at competitive pricing without compromising on quality.

Questions clients ask

Before you appoint an architect

Straight answers to the questions we hear on the first call. Anything else, ask us on WhatsApp.

Ask on WhatsApp
Do you handle both design and execution?

Yes. Architectural design and build services are led by Arcus, with MEP and building engineering delivered through partner support under our coordination. You get one team from concept to handover, or design-only if that is what the project needs.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Food courts and QSR outlets, restaurants and cafés, retail stores and mall interiors, corporate offices, homes, guest houses and highway or wayside destinations, from a single kiosk to a four-acre masterplan.

Where do you work?

The studio is in Sector 63, Noida. Projects in the portfolio span Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Lucknow, Kolkata, Zirakpur and Panchkula, Dehradun, Himachal Pradesh and highway sites across Uttar Pradesh. We travel for site work wherever the project is.

How does the process work, and how long does it take?

Three stages: Design (site study, brief, concept and 3D views), Engineer (detailed drawings, MEP, compliance and tender documents) and Execute (vendor coordination, site supervision and handover). Timelines depend on the size of the site and the approvals involved; after the first site visit we give you a stage-wise programme in writing.

How do you keep a project on budget?

By deciding early. Materials, lighting and MEP are resolved in the drawings, so there are fewer surprises on site. Strategic project management then tracks cost against the estimate through delivery, and we optimise without cutting the design that made you appoint us.

Can you work to our brand guidelines or franchise standards?

Yes, that is most of our work. We have delivered outlets for international and national brands where the identity is fixed and the site is not, and we build template stores that make multi-city rollouts faster.

Do you provide HVAC, electrical and fire-fighting design?

Yes. HVAC layouts and equipment selection, electrical design including load sheets and panel specifications, and fire detection and suppression to the relevant codes, coordinated with the architecture rather than added afterwards.

Will we see the design before anything is built?

Always. Every project is modelled and rendered from the vantage points that matter, up to fourteen camera views on larger schemes, so you approve a space you have effectively walked through.

Do you offer Vastu consultation?

Yes. Our Vastu consultant advises on Vedic, Pyra, Maha and DNA Vastu with practical, result-oriented guidance that works without demolition, integrated with the design rather than imposed on it.

What do you need from us to get started?

The site location, the approximate area in square feet, the brand or use, your target timeline and any drawings you already have. Send that on WhatsApp or through the form below and we will come back with a clear next step.
