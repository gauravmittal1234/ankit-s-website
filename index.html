The studio

One team, from concept to completion.

Arcus Design Build is an architecture and interior design-build studio based in Noida. We integrate sophisticated architectural vision with robust engineering to create high-performing commercial and residential environments: food courts, restaurants and cafés, retail, offices, homes and highway destinations.

The studio is led by Principal Architect Ankit Pandey, who brings more than fifteen years of experience across corporate, residential, retail and hospitality projects, along with international compliance auditing and consultancy across Japan and South Korea.

Our approach is data-driven and aesthetic-led. We use the latest design technologies and sustainable materials so that every project is future-ready, brand-aligned and delivered at competitive pricing without compromising on quality.

What we do

End-to-end design and engineering, under one roof.

Creativity, functionality and efficiency combined, so you deal with one accountable partner instead of five consultants.

01

Architecture

Comprehensive plans from concept to construction, resolved for site, code and budget.

  • General arrangement plans and civil layouts
  • External and internal elevations
  • Demarcation plans and byelaw calculations
  • Site feasibility studies: site conditions, zoning, accessibility and services
  • Master planning for highway and mixed-use sites
02

Interior Design

Interiors that raise both the aesthetic appeal and the day-to-day functionality of a space.

  • Furniture layouts and space planning
  • Finishes and material selection
  • False-ceiling and lighting design
  • Detailed interior elevations and 3D views
  • Brand template stores for multi-city rollouts
03

MEP Services

Mechanical, electrical and fire systems engineered for comfort, efficiency and compliance.

  • HVAC layouts and equipment selection
  • Electrical design: power distribution, lighting layouts, load sheets and panel specifications
  • Fire detection and suppression: sprinklers, alarms, extinguishers and emergency lighting
  • Designed to relevant codes and regulations
04

Project Management

Strategic delivery that optimises cost without sacrificing design integrity.

  • Tender documentation and estimates
  • Vendor coordination and site supervision
  • Quality assurance and progress tracking
  • Seamless transition from concept to completion and handover

Architectural design and build services are led by Arcus. MEP and building engineering services are delivered with partner support.

The Arcus philosophy

Design. Engineer. Execute.

A single, accountable workflow that carries a brief from the first site visit to the day the doors open.

01

Design

We start with the site, the brand and the numbers. Concepts are developed with a data-driven, aesthetic-led methodology using the latest design technologies and sustainable materials, so the outcome is future-ready and brand-aligned.

02

Engineer

Sophisticated architectural vision is integrated with robust engineering: detailed drawings, HVAC, electrical and fire-fighting design, and compliance checks, all coordinated before work begins on site.

03

Execute

Uncompromising quality through strategic project management. We optimise cost without sacrificing design integrity and stay with the project through handover.
